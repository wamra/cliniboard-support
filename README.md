# Cliniboard — Support

**Cliniboard** is a personal clinical health dashboard for iPhone and iPad.  
It reads your Apple Health clinical records and turns them into a clear, private dashboard — labs, vitals, trends, and conditions — all on your device.

## Getting Help

- **Bug reports & questions** → [Open an issue](https://github.com/wamra/cliniboard-support/issues)

## Features

- Lab results organized by panel (CBC, metabolic, lipid, thyroid, and more)
- Blood pressure and eGFR trend charts
- At-a-glance status cards with reference-range indicators
- Conditions list from your clinical records
- Demo Mode — try the app with sample data, no setup required
- iPad support with full-screen optimized layout

## Privacy

Cliniboard does **not** collect, transmit, or store any of your health data.  
Everything stays on your device. No accounts. No servers. No tracking.
