# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** May 30, 2026  
**Last updated:** May 30, 2026

## Who We Are

Cliniboard is developed and operated by the developer of Cliniboard ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the Cliniboard iOS application ("the App") handles your information.

---

## The Short Version

**Cliniboard collects no personal data, transmits no data to any server, and has no user accounts.** The data the App reads stays on your device — and leaves it only if **you** choose to share it (see Sharing below).

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or receive:

- Your name, email address, or any other contact information
- Your Apple Health data, clinical records, lab results, or any health information
- Location data
- Device identifiers or analytics
- Crash reports or usage telemetry
- Cookies or tracking identifiers of any kind

---

## 2. Apple HealthKit Data

The App requests access to your Apple HealthKit data — specifically clinical health records, lab results, vital signs, and conditions — solely to display your personal health dashboard on your device.

This data is:

- **Read-only** — Cliniboard never writes to Apple Health
- **Processed on-device** — it never leaves your iPhone or iPad unless you choose to share it (see Section 4)
- **Not sent to us** — it is never transmitted to our servers or to any third-party service by the App
- **Not used for advertising** — HealthKit data is never used to derive advertising profiles or for any purpose unrelated to displaying your dashboard

This use complies with [Apple's HealthKit guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/health-fitness/).

---

## 3. Data Storage

All data displayed in Cliniboard originates from Apple Health and remains within Apple's secure HealthKit data store and the App's local storage on your device. We do not maintain any databases, servers, or cloud storage of any kind.

---

## 4. Sharing (User-Initiated)

Cliniboard lets you export your dashboard as a `.clini` file that **you** choose to send to others (for example, via AirDrop, Messages, Mail, or Files). This happens only when you explicitly initiate it.

- A `.clini` file is **not encrypted** and contains the full lab and clinical data. The optional obfuscation hides your name, date of birth, and age, but it does **not** remove the health information.
- Only share a file with people you trust, and only if you have the right and permission to share that person's data.
- Sharing is device-to-device through the apps you choose. We have no servers and **never receive** the file. Once you send it, where it goes is under your control, not ours.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

Cliniboard does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-sharing services. No SDKs that collect user data are included in the App.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Because we collect no data from anyone, this policy applies equally to all users.

---

## 7. Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete, export, or correct on our end. If you wish to remove Cliniboard's access to your health data, you can revoke permission at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Cliniboard**.

---

## 8. California Residents (CCPA)

We do not sell personal information. We do not collect personal information. No further CCPA disclosures are required.

---

## 9. European Users (GDPR)

The App reads and displays your health data locally on your device, under your control. We operate no servers and never receive your personal data, so we do not act as a data controller or processor of it under the GDPR. Any processing that occurs happens on your own device. If you choose to share a file, that sharing is initiated and controlled by you.
