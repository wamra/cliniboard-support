# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** May 28, 2026  
**Last updated:** May 28, 2026

## Who We Are

Cliniboard is developed and operated by Wesam Abdallah ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the Cliniboard iOS application ("the App") handles your information.

---

## The Short Version

**Cliniboard collects no personal data, transmits no data to any server, and has no user accounts.** Everything the App reads stays on your device.

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share:

- Your name, email address, or any other contact information
- Your Apple Health data, clinical records, lab results, or any health information
- Location data
- Device identifiers or analytics
- Crash reports or usage telemetry
- Cookies or tracking identifiers of any kind

---

## 2. Apple HealthKit Data

The App requests access to your Apple HealthKit data — specifically clinical health records, lab results, vital signs, and conditions — solely to display your personal health dashboard on your device.

This data is:

- **Read-only** — Cliniboard never writes to Apple Health
- **Processed on-device** — it never leaves your iPhone or iPad
- **Not shared** — it is never transmitted to our servers, third-party services, or any other party
- **Not used for advertising** — HealthKit data is never used to derive advertising profiles or for any purpose unrelated to displaying your dashboard

This use complies with [Apple's HealthKit guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/health-fitness/).

---

## 3. Data Storage

All data displayed in Cliniboard originates from Apple Health and remains within Apple's secure HealthKit data store on your device. We do not maintain any databases, servers, or cloud storage of any kind.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

Cliniboard does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-sharing services. No SDKs that collect user data are included in the App.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Because we collect no data from anyone, this policy applies equally to all users.

---

## 6. Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete, export, or correct on our end. If you wish to remove Cliniboard's access to your health data, you can revoke permission at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Cliniboard**.

---

## 7. California Residents (CCPA)

We do not sell personal information. We do not collect personal information. No further CCPA disclosures are required.

---

## 8. European Users (GDPR)

We do not process personal data as defined under the GDPR. No lawful basis for processing is required because no processing occurs.
