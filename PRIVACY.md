# Cliniboard — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-26_

Cliniboard ("the app") is a personal clinical health dashboard. This policy explains what
data the app accesses, how it is used, and how it is stored. **In short: your health data
stays on your device. We have no servers and never receive, transmit, or sell your data.**

## What data the app accesses

With your explicit permission, Cliniboard reads health data from Apple Health (HealthKit),
which may include:

- Lab results, vital signs, conditions, and allergies (Apple Health Records / clinical records)
- Vitals and related measurements

The app requests **read-only** access. It never writes to or modifies your Apple Health data.

## How the data is used

Health data is used solely to display your personal dashboard on your device — organizing
your labs, vitals, and conditions into a readable summary for you and the clinicians you
choose to show it to.

## How the data is stored

- All data is processed and stored **only on your device** (in the app's private storage).
- There is **no backend server**, **no cloud sync**, and **no account**.
- Your health data is **never transmitted off your device** by the app.

## Data sharing

- We do **not** share, sell, rent, or disclose your health data to anyone.
- Health data is **never** used for advertising or marketing, and is never used to build a
  profile for purposes unrelated to your own health review.
- Any sharing of your dashboard (e.g., showing or printing it for a clinician) is initiated
  and controlled entirely by you.

## Your control

- You grant and revoke the app's access to specific health categories at any time in the
  iOS **Health app → Sharing**, or in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Health**.
- Deleting the app removes all data it stored on your device.

## Not medical advice

Cliniboard is an informational tool. It is **not** medical advice, not a diagnosis, and must
not be used to make treatment decisions. Always consult qualified healthcare providers.

## Children

Cliniboard is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
